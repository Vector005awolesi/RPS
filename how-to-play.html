RPS

How to play and rules



The game is based on the physical rock paper scissors game

  • There are three buttons rock , paper and scissors

  • --- ROCK

  • --- PAPER

  • --- SCISSORS


    • Winning conditions

  • Rock beats scissors
  • Rock and Rock is a draw
  • Rock loses to paper
  • Paper beats rock
  • Paper and Paper is a draw
  • Paper loses to scissors
  • Scissors beats Paper
  • Scissors and Scissors is a draw
  • Scissors loses to rock


    • There is a total of 5 rounds , you and the computer gets to play 10 times ,whoever wins the first 10 plays wins the round and whoever wins all rounds wins the game.

    However the computer is not easy to beat good luck!
